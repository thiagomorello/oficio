## Art

- All tiles, furniture, walls, floors and avatars are drawn procedurally by the code in `client/src/art/`.
- The paintings and posters in `client/public/assets/art/` were generated with image-generation AI and post-processed
  with `scripts/assets/pixelops.py`. They are released with the rest of the project under the MIT license. Replace or
  remove them freely (`client/public/assets/manifest.json`).
- The default logo and icons in `client/public/assets/brand/` are generated by `scripts/assets/make-brand.py`.
